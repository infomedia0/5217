# 5217 v2 — Focus and Rest Quotes

15 quotes per mode, bundled for offline use. Automatic rotation: every 60 seconds while the app is open, including while paused. Next quote (⌘N) changes the quote immediately and restarts its 60-second interval. Each mode remembers its position until the app quits. The timer is unaffected.

## Sources and editorial notes

These short selections are from classic public-domain works and older translations. Linked online editions were checked on 21 October 2026. Excerpts are identified below; closing punctuation and initial capitalization are normalized where a clause is displayed alone. Poetry line breaks are preserved in the app. The author link on each quote opens its source in your browser. John Lubbock’s two selections are sourced through Quote Catalog’s listing for The Use of Life.

## Rest

1. “Rest is not idleness.” — **John Lubbock**  
   [The Use of Life (1894), excerpt](https://www.quotecatalog.com/source/the-use-of-life-1894)

2. “I love a broad margin to my life.” — **Henry David Thoreau**  
   [Walden, Sounds (1854)](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/205/pg205-images.html)

3. “Nothing can bring you peace but yourself.” — **Ralph Waldo Emerson**  
   [Self-Reliance, Essays: First Series (1841)](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/2944/2944-h/2944-h.htm)

4. “But in every walk with Nature one receives far more than he seeks.” — **John Muir**  
   [Steep Trails, Mormon Lilies (1918)](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/326/326-h/326-h.htm)

5. “Come forth into the light of things, / Let Nature be your teacher.” — **William Wordsworth**  
   [The Tables Turned (1798), excerpt](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/45557/the-tables-turned)

6. “To sit in the shade on a fine day, and look upon verdure, is the most perfect refreshment.” — **Jane Austen**  
   [Mansfield Park, chapter IX (1814), Fanny Price speaking; excerpt](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/141/141-h/141-h.htm)

7. “What is this life if, full of care, / We have no time to stand and stare.” — **W. H. Davies**  
   [Leisure, Songs of Joy and Others (1911), opening lines](https://rpo.library.utoronto.ca/content/leisure)

8. “Heaven is under our feet as well as over our heads.” — **Henry David Thoreau**  
   [Walden, The Pond in Winter (1854)](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/205/pg205-images.html)

9. “A day of worry is more exhausting than a week of work.” — **John Lubbock**  
   [The Use of Life (1894)](https://www.quotecatalog.com/source/the-use-of-life-1894)

10. “Leave this military hurry and adopt the pace of Nature.” — **Ralph Waldo Emerson**  
   [Education, Lectures and Biographical Sketches (1883); Emerson concordance](https://concordlibrary.org/special-collections/emerson-concordance/PAA-PARABL)

11. “We need the tonic of wildness.” — **Henry David Thoreau**  
   [Walden, Spring (1854), excerpt](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/205/pg205-images.html)

12. “A thing of beauty is a joy for ever.” — **John Keats**

13. “Constantly then give to thyself this retreat, and renew thyself.” — **Marcus Aurelius**  
   [Meditations IV.3, translated by George Long (1862), excerpt](https://classics.mit.edu/Antoninus/meditations.4.four.html)

14. “We can never have enough of Nature.” — **Henry David Thoreau**  
   [Walden, Spring (1854)](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/205/pg205-images.html)

15. “Come forth, and bring with you a heart / That watches and receives.” — **William Wordsworth**
